The big idea

Gravity is constantly accelerating the planet toward the star. But the planet also has sideways velocity. Instead of moving in a straight line forever, its path continually bends toward the star.

F = GMm / r²

Notice the r². Double the distance and the gravitational force becomes four times weaker. That's one reason distance matters so much in orbital motion.
